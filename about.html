About Hills Cafe

Naihati's cafe with the pink sign in the window.

A first-floor spot on Rishi Bankim Chandra Road that mixes momos, pizza, Chinese and continental plates under a ceiling of hanging stars — open every day from late morning to late evening.

Hills Cafe sits one floor up on Rishi Bankim Chandra Road, easy to spot once the sun goes down by the pink neon sign glowing in its front window. Inside, red leather stools and marble-top tables run the length of the room, and a canopy of hanging stars, a paper moon, and warm string lights covers the ceiling — the kind of detail that turns a quick bite into people staying for a second round of momos.

The kitchen doesn't stick to one cuisine. Momos and Chinese noodles sit on the same menu as wood-fired pizza, pasta and tandoor chicken, which makes it an easy pick for a mixed table of orders. It's a walk-in kind of place, but calling ahead helps if you're coming with a group.

The space

Marble floors, red leather seating, and a ceiling that does most of the talking.
